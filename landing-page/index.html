What it does

One video in. Five study tools out.

Every feature is designed to make you test yourself — not just re-read notes. That's the whole philosophy.

📝

Searchable transcript

Whisper transcribes every video. Click any line to jump the video. Live search with prev/next navigation. Export to MD, JSON, or TXT.

🧠

Clickable mindmap

Every node is a timestamp. Click → video jumps. Even leaf nodes work — the AI walks up the tree to find the nearest parent.

📇

Auto flashcards + quiz

Not just summaries — actual recall tests. Click "Teach me real-world usage" on any flashcard to open a tutor chat.

💬

Discuss the whole video

A ChatGPT-style chat with the full transcript + summary + mindmap as context. Ask "what did the prof mean at 23:15?" — it answers.

⏱️

Topic timestamps

Auto-generated by the LLM. Jump back to the exact 3-minute segment you need to review. No more scrubbing.
