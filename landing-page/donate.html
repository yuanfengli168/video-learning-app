FAQ

Is this tax-deductible?

No. I'm a solo developer, not a registered non-profit. You won't get a tax receipt. If you need a tax-deductible donation, please consider donating to a registered open-source foundation like the Eclipse Foundation instead.

Where does the money actually go?

Honestly: hosting, domain names, and the occasional hardware upgrade (a faster Mac for testing transcode perf). I don't take a salary from this — it's a side project. Anything extra goes toward the next feature (a Mac mini cluster for cloud-friendly hosting, someday).

Can I donate in cryptocurrency?

Not directly — but if crypto is your thing, email me and I'll set up a wallet. ETH, USDC, BTC all work for me.

Will you add me to the Supporters wall?

Only if you want me to. Check the wall — by default donors show as "Anonymous" unless they opt in. Email me with your preferred name + a 1-line comment and I'll add you on the next update.

Is it safe to post QR codes publicly? Privacy & safety notes

Short answer: yes, with the caveats below. A payment QR code is just a payment address — like an email address for money. It doesn't expose your bank login, password, or balance.

What I personally do to stay safe:

  • Use a dedicated email for donations (this site's contact address is already dedicated to the project). If you have a personal Zelle tied to your main phone, consider using a Google Voice number or a separate payments-only email.
  • PayNow is by NRIC/UEN — slightly more identifiable than an email, but it's the only way PayNow works in SG.
  • WeChat Pay / Alipay display your avatar/nickname to me by default. If you'd rather stay anonymous, ask me to set up a "收款码" (collection-only QR) without a profile.
  • Push payments (Zelle, PayNow) cannot be reversed — don't send money to anyone you haven't verified. I will never DM you asking for a donation.

If you spot a problem (a scammer impersonating me, a phishing link, a leaked credential) please email jackyopenclaw.168@gmail.com.
